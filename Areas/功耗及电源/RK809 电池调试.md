### 充电 dts 配置
```c
          stat_charger: stat-charger {
                  compatible = "gpio-charger";
                  charger-type = "mains";
                  pinctrl-names = "default";
                  pinctrl-0 = <&det_stat_gpio>;
                  //charge-status-gpios = <&gpio3 RK_PA6 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>;
                  gpios = <&gpio3 RK_PC1 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>;
                  status = "okay";
          };
```

- 内核配置：
  - @  RK809 没有充电功能，所以 charger 节点不用配置，但需要把 BQ25700 的节点打开
