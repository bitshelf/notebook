## 基础
- **procfs** exposes information about processes and system（`man proc(5)`）
- **sysfs** filesystem exposes information about various kernel subsystems, hardware devices and association with drivers `(man sysfs`(5))
- **[[debugfs]]** is a simple RAM-based filesystem which exposes debugging information
